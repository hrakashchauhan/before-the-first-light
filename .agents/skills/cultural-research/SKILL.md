# Cultural research skill

Classify every detail as verified-local, common-but-variable, family-specific, needs-review, fictional-placeholder or not-for-release. Ask who confirmed it and in what context. Do not convert one family's practice into a universal claim. Require native-speaker review for Bhojpuri and explicit rights/consent for audio and likeness. Remove uncertain details from release rather than guessing.
