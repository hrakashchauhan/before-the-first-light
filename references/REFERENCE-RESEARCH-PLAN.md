# Reference research plan

Collect references by category, not as a single moodboard:
1. Bihar village house/courtyard construction and proportions.
2. Local courtyard surfaces, walls, roofs, doors and thresholds.
3. Goitha shapes, placement and storage in the selected locality.
4. Clay chulha form and safe use.
5. Wheat cleaning/drying/storage methods.
6. Bamboo artisan process and soop/daura construction.
7. Pond/ghat architecture and access.
8. Clothing and everyday household objects.
9. Chhath offering arrangements and local variation.
10. Dawn lighting, water reflections and crowd spacing.

For every reference record photographer/creator, URL/source, date accessed, permission/licence and locality. Reference images are for study only; do not use them as in-game photo backgrounds or textures. Do not copy a living artist's distinctive style.
