## 1. Guiding Principles
1. **Dignity over Spectacle:** Chhath Puja is portrayed through quiet dedication, family cooperation, and community harmony rather than sensationalized game mechanics.
2. **No False Universals:** Portrays one specific fictional family and locality in rural Bihar; never claims one household's recipe, song, or routine is universal across all regions.
3. **Shared Labor:** The mother (Vrati) is supported by returning family members, elders, father, children, and neighbors who take on the physical labor of cleaning, carrying, sorting, and ghat preparation.
4. **Safety & Reverence:** Fasting, fire handling, and dehydration are never presented as health meters, score challenges, or survival tasks.
