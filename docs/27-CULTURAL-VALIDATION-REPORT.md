## 1. Upgraded Cultural Representation (Inhabited Household)

1. **The Vrati Mother (Ma):**
   - Draped in a traditional yellow/saffron cotton saree (*peela rang*) with sacred red border (*lal kinari*), head respectfully veiled by the *anchal*.
   - Portrayed with dignity, standing near the veranda cooking zone, offering gentle maternal guidance rather than serving as a detached quest-dispenser.
   - Dialogue reinforces that preparations are a shared familial effort, not an isolated burden.
2. **The Returning Family Member (Player Character - Devraj):**
   - Dressed in everyday clean unbleached cotton kurta-pajama.
   - Takes on the physical labor of cleaning, carrying, inspecting, and protecting materials so the Vrati can maintain ritual sanctity and rest.
3. **The Courtyard Broom (Kharhara / Sikke ki Jhadu):**
   - Authentic hand-tied straw broom used across Bihar villages to sweep courtyards clean of dust and debris before spreading sacred wheat.
4. **The Village Handpump (Chapakal):**
   - Recognizable cast-iron handpump with concrete drainage apron, representing the shared groundwater lifeblood of rural households.
5. **The Woven Charpai (Khatia):**
   - Four-legged wooden cot with jute cord webbing and folded cotton quilt, placed in the courtyard for rest and conversation.
