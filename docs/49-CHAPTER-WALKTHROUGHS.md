## Overview

The game follows an authentic four-day Chhath Puja festival in rural Bihar, spanning the preparatory days through to the sacred dawn conclusion.

---

### Chapter 0: Homecoming (घर वापसी)
- **Time:** Morning
- **Primary Location:** Family Courtyard
- **Characters Present:** Ma (Shanti Devi), Player (Devraj)
- **Objective:** Arrive at the village home and greet Ma on the veranda.
- **Player Tasks:** Walk across the courtyard, approach the veranda, press `[E]` to talk.
- **Audio:** Supplied soundtrack intro theme ("Chhathi Maiya Bulaye"), morning birds ambience.
- **Completion:** Unlocks Chapter 1 and reveals the courtyard broom.

### Chapter 1: Household Preparation (आँगन की तैयारी)
- **Time:** Morning
- **Primary Location:** Courtyard & Veranda
- **Objective:** Help sanctify the open earth where sacred prasad preparations will occur.
- **Player Tasks:**
  1. Pick up grass broom at veranda corner.
  2. Clean leaves and debris beside the drying mat.
  3. Speak with Ma to confirm courtyard readiness.
- **Family Activity:** Ma washes brass utensils; Bauji checks dry firewood for the chulha.
- **Completion:** Courtyard ground turns freshly swept; unlocks bicycle outside courtyard gate.

### Chapter 2: Fields and Resources (खेत और रसद)
- **Time:** Afternoon
- **Primary Location:** Village Lane & Field Edge
- **Objective:** Travel along the lane on bicycle to check on wheat drying.
- **Player Tasks:**
  1. Approach parked bicycle near courtyard lane gate (`[5.2, 0, 3.8]`).
  2. Press `[E]` to Mount Bicycle.
  3. Ride along the open village road towards the sunny field stretch.
  4. Dismount and spread clean golden wheat grains evenly across the woven mat.
  5. Mount bicycle and ride back to the household.
- **Audio:** Travel ambience, bicycle chain/pedal ticking, gentle wind through roadside neem trees.
- **Completion:** Wheat mat displays spread golden grain; unlocks Chapter 3.

### Chapter 3: Market and Artisans (हाट और बाँस के कारीगर)
- **Time:** Late Afternoon
- **Primary Location:** Artisan Workshop (Mohan Kaka)
- **Objective:** Visit the village artisan to collect handmade bamboo *soop* and *daura*.
- **Player Tasks:**
  1. Ride bicycle down the village lane to Mohan Kaka's roadside workshop (`[12.5, 0.2, 5.4]`).
  2. Park bicycle in the designated artisan parking bay.
  3. Speak with Mohan Kaka about traditional weaving techniques and ritual sanctity.
  4. Collect the woven soop and securely place them on the bicycle rear carrier.
  5. Ride back home and deliver the baskets to Ma.
- **Family Activity:** Ma carefully inspects the baskets, washing them with clean water.
- **Completion:** Unlocks Chapter 4.

### Chapter 4: Community Preparation (गाँव का सहयोग)
- **Time:** Evening
- **Primary Location:** Village Lane & Neighbour's Courtyard
- **Objective:** Assist neighbours with earthen *diyas* (clay lamps) and lane cleaning.
- **Player Tasks:**
  1. Ride down the lane to meet Malti Chachi.
  2. Deliver dry cow-dung cakes (*goitha*) for her sacred hearth.
  3. Clear small roadside stones from the procession path leading to the ghat.
- **Community Activity:** Neighbours whitewash tree trunks and erect bamboo torches along the road.
- **Completion:** Procession route to the pond is illuminated; unlocks festival days.

### Chapter 5: Nahay Khay (नहाय खाय — पहला दिन)
- **Time:** Morning
- **Primary Location:** Pond & Household Hearth
- **Objective:** Experience the opening day of ritual purification.
- **Ritual Focus:** Sacred bath in clean water; preparation of pure bottle-gourd curry (*kaddu-bhat*) and arwa rice cooked on clay chulha.
- **Player Tasks:** Accompany family to the pond steps for ceremonial ablutions; bring fresh water home.
- **Audio:** Solemn acoustic drone, water ripples, temple conch.

### Chapter 6: Kharna (खरना — दूसरा दिन)
- **Time:** Evening (After Sunset)
- **Primary Location:** Courtyard & Sacred Kitchen
- **Objective:** Support Ma during her 36-hour nirjala fast and evening worship.
- **Ritual Focus:** Cooking jaggery kheer (*rasiya*) and rotis on the clay stove using mango firewood.
- **Player Tasks:** Maintain absolute silence and purity while Ma offers prasad to Surya Dev and Chhathi Maiya; share rasiya prasad with visiting family and neighbours.

### Chapter 7: Sandhya Arghya (संध्या अर्घ्य — तीसरा दिन)
- **Time:** Late Afternoon / Sunset
- **Primary Location:** Village Pond Ghat
- **Objective:** Accompany the family carrying bamboo *daura* to offer twilight prayers.
- **Player Tasks:**
  1. Ride bicycle to the upper ghat parking area (`[18.0, 0, 3.2]`).
  2. Park and dismount (bicycles restricted on ritual steps).
  3. Walk barefoot down the brick steps to the water's edge.
  4. Assist Bauji in holding the brass *lota* while Ma stands waist-deep in water offering milk to the setting sun.
- **Audio:** Deep devotional score, gentle evening cicadas, sacred mantras.

### Chapter 8: Night Before Dawn (कोसी भराई)
- **Time:** Night
- **Primary Location:** Courtyard & Pond Ghat
- **Objective:** Participate in the nocturnal vigil of prayer and singing (*Koshi Bharai*).
- **Ritual Focus:** Earthen elephants surrounded by sugarcane stalks with 24 clay lamps lit under the canopy.
- **Player Tasks:** Keep clay lamps burning with mustard oil; listen to Dadi's traditional folk ballads (*Chhathi Maiya Bulaye*).

### Chapter 9: Usha Arghya (उषा अर्घ्य — चौथा दिन)
- **Time:** Pre-Dawn to Sunrise
- **Primary Location:** Pond Ghat (Dawn Steps)
- **Objective:** Stand with the community in the cool water awaiting the first light of day.
- **Climax:** As the crimson disc of the rising sun pierces the morning mist over the pond, Ma offers the final arghya.
- **Audio:** Full supplied theme crescendo ("Chhathi Maiya Bulaye" master arrangement) with ecstatic community celebration.

### Chapter 10: Parana & Blessing (पारन और आशीष)
- **Time:** Post-Sunrise
- **Primary Location:** Family Veranda
- **Objective:** Break the fast with sacred ginger, raw sugar, and thekua prasad.
- **Player Tasks:**
  1. Receive blessings from Ma, Bauji, and elders.
  2. Distribute handmade thekua prasad to villagers, children, and Mohan Kaka.
  3. Reflect on the collective labor, devotion, and community bonds of Bihar.
