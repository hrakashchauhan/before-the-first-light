# 03 — Cultural research and validation

## Principle
Represent one carefully researched fictional household and locality. Do not claim that one family's sequence, object arrangement, recipe, song or timing represents all Bihar or all Chhath-observing communities.

## Validate with people
Before finalising details, consult where possible:
- A Chhath-observing Vrati.
- An elder familiar with the household's local practice.
- Someone who prepares or uses goitha.
- Someone who builds/uses a clay chulha.
- A bamboo artisan or experienced soop/daura user.
- A local Bhojpuri speaker and folk singer.
- A person involved in ghat/community organisation.

Ask permission before recording or using anyone's voice, likeness, story or music.

## Research ledger
For each cultural detail record:
- Detail being represented.
- Locality/community context.
- Who confirmed it and when (only with consent).
- Whether it is common, variable, family-specific or uncertain.
- What the game will show.
- What must not be generalised.
- Approval status.

## Practices to research, not assume
- Nahay Khay, Kharna, Sandhya Arghya, Usha Arghya and Parana sequence and local interpretation.
- Wheat selection, cleaning, drying, storage and grinding practices.
- Goitha making, placement, drying, storage and use.
- Chulha construction, curing, fuel and safe use.
- Soop and daura materials, weaving and handling.
- Thekua and other prasad preparation.
- Offerings and their local variations.
- Aripan/alpana patterns and their meaning.
- Kosi observance: optional and not universal.
- Local songs, instruments, lyrics and performance context.

## Safety and dignity
- Never reward dehydration or fasting endurance.
- Do not simulate unsafe fire handling as a fun mini-game.
- Do not show children handling flames or hot vessels.
- Show shared labour, rest, care and practical support.
- Keep the ghat clean and safe; avoid dangerous crowding.
- Do not use sacred symbols as random decoration or collectibles.

## Content status labels
`verified-local`, `common-but-variable`, `family-specific`, `needs-review`, `fictional-placeholder`, `not-for-release`.

Any `needs-review` or `not-for-release` item must be resolved or removed before release.
