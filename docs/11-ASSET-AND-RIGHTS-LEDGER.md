## Rules
- No asset enters a release build until its licence is checked.
- A platform's general terms do not replace the licence for an individual asset.
- Record consent for real people's voices, names and likenesses.
- Keep licence files and permission evidence in `references/rights/` where permitted.
- If rights are unclear, replace the asset with original work or remove it.
