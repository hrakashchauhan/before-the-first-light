## 2. Character Profiles

### Devraj (Player Character)
- **Role:** The returning son; active participant in family and community preparation.
- **Visual Design:** Slender young man in soft earth-tone cotton kurta and pajama; procedural walk cycle with responsive turns; carries tools and baskets with physical presence.
- **Personality:** Attentive, respectful, observant. Speaks through thoughtful, grounded dialogue lines.

### Ma (Sunita Devi)
- **Role:** Household matriarch and devout Vrati observing the Mahaparv.
- **Visual Design:** Seated gracefully on the raised veranda (*ooth*); draped in a traditional saffron-bordered cotton saree; sculpted face with calm, loving eyes and vermilion sindoor.
- **Personality:** Patient, deeply affectionate, coordinates the household's preparations. She ensures that preparation is accompanied by goodwill and care.

### Mohan Kaka
- **Role:** Village master bamboo artisan.
- **Visual Design:** Seated cross-legged on a woven chatayi beneath his thatched porch; dressed in a simple khadi kurta and red-checked cotton gamchha; silver-grey hair and mustache; rhythmic arm motions split and weave green bamboo.
- **Personality:** Proud of his craft, wise, warmly welcomes Devraj. Treats the winnowing baskets (*soop*) not merely as merchandise, but as sacred offerings carrying the prayers of the entire village.

### The Community (Neighbours & Ghat Devotees)
- **Role:** Village collective presence.
- **Visual Design:** Groups gathering along the earthen lane, women bearing brass vessels, men carrying dauras on their shoulders, families adorning steps with lime and vermilion.
- **Personality:** Welcoming, mutualistic; embodying the spirit that no single family celebrates Chhath in isolation.

## 3. Bilingual Dialogue Principles

1. **Natural Hindi & English:** Dialogue avoids archaic melodrama and modern corporate idioms.
2. **Key Cultural Terminology Retained:**
   - *Ma* (Mother)
   - *Kaka* (Paternal Uncle / Elder Artisan)
   - *Soop* (Winnowing basket)
   - *Daura* (Deep bamboo offering basket)
   - *Ooth* (Raised earthen veranda)
   - *Thekua* (Sacred wheat and jaggery prasad)
   - *Ghat* (Terraced waterbank steps)
   - *Pokhra* (Village pond)
   - *Prasad* (Blessed offering food)
3. **Dialogue Delivery:** Responsive on-screen dialogue overlay with clear speaker identification, prominent typography, and keyboard/mouse advancement.
