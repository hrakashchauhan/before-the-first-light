# 00 — Project vision

## One-sentence vision
Let players experience the care, preparation, cooperation and emotional meaning that lead a Bihar village towards the first sunrise of Chhath Puja.

## Player promise
The player does not merely arrive at the ghat for the final ritual. They discover the many days of work that make that sunrise possible.

## Pillars
1. **A living place:** the village changes as preparation advances.
2. **Meaningful small work:** cleaning, sorting, shaping, carrying, preparing and helping have visible consequences.
3. **Human relationships:** family, neighbours, artisans and elders make the village feel inhabited.
4. **Cultural respect:** rituals and dialogue are reviewed, locally grounded and never flattened into a single universal rule.
5. **A memorable dawn:** the emotional climax is earned through the player's accumulated contribution.

## Audience and tone
- Broad audience, including players unfamiliar with Chhath.
- Warm, grounded, observant, hopeful and intimate.
- Not a combat game, not a festival-management spreadsheet, not a caricature of rural life.

## Scope
MVP: one compact village zone, one household courtyard, one lane, a small market edge, pond/ghat, a few NPCs, a short preparation sequence and the sunrise ending.

Later expansion: additional households, more village routines, seasonal weather, optional stories, more language coverage and richer animation.

## Success measures
- A new player understands the central task within 60 seconds.
- The first meaningful interaction works without a tutorial wall of text.
- The player can complete a 10–15 minute vertical slice.
- The village visibly changes after completed tasks.
- The final dawn scene feels connected to earlier actions.
- No critical cultural issue remains unresolved in the released content.
